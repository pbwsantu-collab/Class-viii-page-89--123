Topic 1: Poem Text

Someone

Walter de la Mare

Let's read:

Someone came knocking
At my wee small door;
Someone came knocking,
I'm sure- sure- sure;
I listened, I opened,
I looked to left and right,
But nought there was a-stirring
In the still, dark night.

A Mysterious Visitor
It was a dark and quiet night. I was reading a book alone in my room. Suddenly, I heard a soft knock at the front door. Curious yet nervous, I held my candle and went to open it. I opened the door wide and looked left and right into the darkness, but no one was there. Only the gentle night wind rustled the leaves. Realizing it was just the wind knocking loose branches against the door frame, I smiled, closed the door, and returned safely to my desk.
